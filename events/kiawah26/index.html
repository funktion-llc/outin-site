How this runs — the Abacus Cup way, supercharged

This is the same process that worked with Matty on the Abacus Cup in Google Drive — intake sheet, discovery brief, written plan, agreement, build, test round — except the hub replaces the scattered Drive folders and email threads. Same filing underneath, one front door on top: everything you tap here is sent to John and filed into the official Project Plan and Brief, so the copy in Drive stays current and nothing exists twice.

  1. Intake (done). Trip basics captured and filed under OUTIN-001 — dates, courses, group size.
  2. Quick fill (this hub). Your known basics, roster, notes, and upgrade interests become the starting brief — no spreadsheet to fill out first. Anything left open is flagged “cover on the call.”
  3. Planning call. We walk the flagged items live, settle handicaps and stakes, and agree the scope. The call intake and transcript join your answers as they come in.
  4. Docs spin out in Drive. The call intake, transcript, and your taps generate the official Plan, Brief, and Breakdown in the shared folder — and the confirmed format posts here in the hub, so the app and the filed docs never drift apart.
  5. Project Plan for approval. A branded plan doc lands in Files with formats, schedule, and line-item pricing (base + any upgrades you flagged, finalized). You review, request changes, or approve.
  6. Agreement + deposit. Client Services Agreement signed, deposit in — the build clock starts.
  7. Design, then build. Screen previews post to Files for your thumbs-up before code. Then scoring, games, and payout math get built.
  8. Test round. Your group plays a trial round on the real app before the trip so tee-day has no surprises.
  9. Trip live — Oct 22–24. Live leaderboard, side games, and settle-up in the app. End-of-trip recap files back to this hub.

Where Drive worked fine (shared docs, signatures, invoices), we keep it — those files surface in Files here, tagged OUTIN-001. The hub is the conversation; Drive remains the filing cabinet.
